# Aptean account ownership, and the email to Jason Roof

## The situation

Aptean is claimed in Monex's CRM by **Jason Roof**. Marcel discovered
this on 2026-09-23 while going to register the account after booking the
Oct 13 meeting with Clifford D'Souza.

## What the CRM record actually shows

**Jason Roof's recent activity:**
- Email to **Clifford D'Souza**, 2026-07-31, marked **Unopened**
- Task from 2026-07-20, **still overdue**: "Contact Aptean (had meeting
  w/ Kantox) / John Meade"
- BDR calling sequence to Hiroshi Tanaka, 16 steps, June 2026, no answer
  at any step

**Legacy history**, showing how long this account has circled without
landing: cold calls June 2024 to Tehmina Irfan, Susan Ricker in treasury
and Rahul Radhakrishnan, all unanswered. Notes from 2019 naming Kevin
McAdams as CFO and Brad DeBold as SVP Corporate Development, with the
account passing between Helen Given, Alexander Stolz and others.

**The fact worth keeping for the desk, not for the email:** Jason's
direct email to Clifford was never opened. Marcel's LinkedIn message
reached the same person and drew a reply in three hours, six weeks later.

## How Marcel is playing it

Going to Jason first, before any manager, and before Jason could learn it
from the CRM or from Clifford. That is the move that keeps this a
courtesy rather than a conflict.

**Deliberately left out of the email:**

- **That Jason never got a meeting.** True, and the reason Marcel feels
  entitled to a share, but in writing it reads as *I did what you could
  not*. The CRM says it without Marcel pointing.
- **Any ranking of himself below Jason.** An early draft said "you know
  more FX than I do" and considered mentioning that English is not
  Marcel's first language. Both hand a rival a reason to take the lead
  and cost standing that will be wanted later. Credit what Jason brings
  without grading yourself.
- **A pre-offered account split.** Account ownership and comp have rules
  Marcel may not be able to commit unilaterally. "How do you want to
  handle it" is stronger and often produces a better answer than
  conceding half up front.
- **The date of the LinkedIn outreach**, at Marcel's decision on
  2026-09-23. The honesty is carried by "I had not realised before
  today", which is true. A courtesy note does not owe a colleague a
  timeline.

**The separate obligation:** the CRM is a record, not a narrative. Log
the LinkedIn activity accurately when registering. Omitting a detail from
an email and misdating a regulated firm's activity log are different
orders of thing.

If Jason does see the date, it is not damaging. Prospecting first and
registering on traction is ordinary practice.

## Held back for the conversation, not the email

**Offering Jason the lead on the call.** The strongest team player signal
available, more likely to be declined or halved than accepted, and worth
more said out loud than written down. If he does take it, Marcel is still
in the room on an account he sourced.

**The prep note.** Offered in the email, sent only once Jason replies.

## If it goes quiet

Two business days, then one line: *"Following up on the below, mainly so
we are not both in front of them separately."*

If still quiet, go to a manager with a **question, not a complaint**:
*"Jason owns Aptean, I have a meeting booked, how would you like us to
handle it?"* That is a process question and it is very hard to answer
badly.

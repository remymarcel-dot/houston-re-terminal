# Memo: asking a senior colleague onto the Aptean call

Paste ready. Adjust the one line flagged at the bottom depending on
whether the recipient is a peer, a specialist or Marcel's manager.

---

**Subject: Oct 13, Aptean treasury call. $1B in payments across 100 entities, and I would like you on it.**

I have a call booked with Aptean on **Tuesday 13 October at 10:00 ET**
and I would like you to join me.

**Who is on it**

Clifford D'Souza, Treasury Director, based in Atlanta. He is bringing
Madhusudan N, his Treasury Manager, who joins from Bengaluru at 19:30
their time. Both treasury seats on one call, and the operational one
staying late for it.

**Why it is worth your hour**

Aptean is an ERP software group out of Alpharetta, owned by four private
equity firms, TA Associates leading since 2022. They have made **more
than 55 acquisitions since 2018** and serve 10,000 customers in over 50
countries.

Their Treasury Manager describes the operation publicly as **$50M daily
liquidity, over $1B in payments, 150+ bank accounts, 100+ entities and
10+ banks**.

**How it came about**

I wrote to Clifford on LinkedIn on 21 September, saying that growth by
acquisition usually leaves treasury holding a currency book nobody
designed. He replied within three hours asking to talk and sent his
email unprompted. He then picked the slot himself and accepted the
invite the same day.

**Why I want you there rather than briefing you after**

This is not a company with a problem it has not noticed. Madhusudan has
spent four years on cash pooling, account rationalisation, payment
automation and a treasury system implementation. They are sophisticated,
and their stated direction is **fewer banking relationships**, which
means we walk in looking like one more.

My read is that the opening is the long tail, the corridors their main
banks serve badly and the acquired entities too small to get attention.
That argument needs someone who can hold it at depth in the room rather
than promise to come back with an answer. That is what I am asking for.

**What I have already done**

Full prep note on the company, both people, the questions and the traps.
I will send it ahead of the call. The aim is not to win the treasury. It
is to leave with three corridors we can price against what they pay now.

Let me know and I will add you to the invite.

Marcel

## The line to adjust

*"Why I want you there rather than briefing you after"* is written for a
**peer or specialist whose expertise Marcel wants**.

If the recipient is **Marcel's manager**, change that heading to
*"Where I would value your read"* and soften the ask from "that is what
I am asking for" to "I would rather have you in the room than summarise
it afterwards." Same content, different power dynamic.

## Do not write "inbound"

Marcel, 2026-09-23: *"do not say inbound as it triggers aditional
compliance scrutiny"*. The word is a lead classification at Monex and
using it loosely in internal writing invites review that the opportunity
does not need.

The facts carry the meaning without it. He replied in three hours, sent
his email unprompted, chose the slot and accepted the invite. Any reader
draws their own conclusion, and nobody has to defend a label.

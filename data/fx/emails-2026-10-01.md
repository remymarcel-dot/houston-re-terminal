I wrote last week about French oak and the cost of a case. Let me give you the

If Lasseter buys its barrels through a US importer who quotes in dollars, the
currency risk has not gone away. It has been priced into the invoice by

four percent. It does not appear as a line item, which is exactly why it
survives.

So the question is not really whether you have euro exposure. It is whether
you are paying for someone else to manage it.

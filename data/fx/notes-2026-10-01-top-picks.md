He **is** the treasurer. Explaining currency risk to him would be the fastest way
to lose him, so the note concedes that in the first line and asks a narrower
question he can answer in one sentence. It also offers him an easy exit, which
is what makes the question credible.

> Syed, we have been connected a while without actually speaking, so this is
> overdue on my side.
>
> You carry CFO and Treasurer together at a global resin business, which means
> you already own this question and do not need me to describe it. So I will ask
> the narrower one: when Americhem buys resin abroad, is the rate set before the
> purchase order or at settlement?
>

> is larger than the hedging program they decided not to build.
>
> I work on FX and cross border payments at Monex USA. If you have the desk
> covered, say so and I will leave it there. If the answer is that it depends who
> is doing the buying, that is worth twenty minutes.
>
> Marcel

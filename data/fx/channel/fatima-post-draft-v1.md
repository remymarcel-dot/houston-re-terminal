## The one gap

She names **recourse factoring** and never defines recourse. A produce
reader does not necessarily know it means the company repays whether or
not its own customer has paid it.

That is the whole difference between this structure and one where the
financier carries buyer risk. Someone arriving expecting the second and
meeting the first would make **her** look wrong to her own audience, and
she has credited Marcel with reviewing the financial details, so it lands
on him too.

Proposed single line, nothing else touched:

> Recourse means the U.S. company repays the financing whether or not its
> own customer has paid.

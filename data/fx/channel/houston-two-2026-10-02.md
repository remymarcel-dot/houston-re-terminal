## 11. Jose Luis Lopez Mota, Managing Director, vOfiz Inc
**Greater Houston · Tec de Monterrey · 49 mutuals · shared LATAM CFO group**

### What vOfiz actually is

Not an account. **vOfiz is an outsourced finance department.** From its own site:
bookkeeping, accounting, tax planning and filing, back-office operations and
corporate compliance, for small businesses. Its Gusto partner profile adds
outsourced CFO, payroll and financial planning, and lists it as serving 9 Gusto
clients, a partner since 2022. Address 10330 Lake Rd Ste F, Houston 77070.

**The origin is the part that matters.** vOfiz was split out of **MX Global
Center** on 1 January 2025. The other half, "North," kept the original business:
guiding international companies into the US market through a "soft landing"
process, including legal and financial navigation, and now turnkey acquisition
and real estate investment.

So vOfiz is the bookkeeping arm of a **Mexico-to-US market entry practice**.

### Why that makes him the best channel find in the screen

vOfiz has essentially no currency exposure of its own. What it has is **a book of
small US entities, a large share of them Mexican owned, every one of which pays
or receives across the border and none of which has anyone watching the rate.**
vOfiz does their bookkeeping, which means vOfiz sees every one of those wires.

Add 49 mutual connections, Tec de Monterrey, and a shared LATAM CFO group. There
is no colder way to describe this than a referral partner who is already holding
the exact client list Marcel wants.

**So the note does not ask about his exposure. It proposes a trade.** Pretending
to pitch a bookkeeping firm on FX risk management would waste the best
introduction on the list.

### Spanish, and I would not hesitate here

Tec de Monterrey, an MX Global Center lineage, a LATAM CFO group, 49 mutuals in
a Mexican professional network. **Spanish is the right call**, and unlike Rosa
Duarte this is not an inference from a surname.

> Jose Luis, vOfiz lleva el back office de empresas pequenas, y buena parte seran
> entidades estadounidenses de dueno mexicano que pagan proveedores del otro
> lado. Eso es justo lo que atiendo en Monex USA. Vale un cafe para ver si
> deberiamos pasarnos trabajo. Marcel

**263 chars.** English version if Marcel prefers, 280 chars:

> those will be Mexican owned US entities paying suppliers across the border.

### One rule to hold

A referral relationship with vOfiz does **not** license approaching its clients
directly. If this works, the introductions come from him. Going around him once
would end it permanently, and he is worth more than any single account in his book.

## 12. Alfredo Amparan Garza, CFO, Workhub Developments LLC
**Greater Houston · 17 mutuals · LinkedIn flagged shared education**

### Three corrections to what the screen recorded

**He is a co-founder, not a hired CFO.** Workhub was founded in 2015 by Roberto
Valenzuela and Alfredo Amparan. His own company bio: he "leads all the company's
financial operations" and "oversees the company's ongoing expansion efforts in
emerging markets."

**Workhub is bigger than the screen assumed.** Fully vertically integrated
small-bay industrial developer, seven projects in Greater Houston since 2015,
30+ tenant companies, and a **strategic investment from Davidson Kempner Capital
Management announced August 2024**, advised by Baird. Four more projects coming
in Magnolia, Spring, Katy and Pflugerville, 127 suites and roughly 256,000 sq ft.

**Headquarters is 1780 Hughes Landing Blvd, Suite 670, The Woodlands TX 77380.**

### So he is a neighbor, and this time that is actually true

Marcel's office is 9595 Six Pines Dr, Suite 8210, The Woodlands 77380. Alfredo's
is 1780 Hughes Landing Blvd, same zip, same side of town, a few minutes apart.

**Recorded deliberately, because this is the claim that went wrong once.** The
Sean Fightmaster draft said "I am five minutes from you" about an address in
north Houston that is a thirty minute drive. Here both addresses are in The
Woodlands 77380 and I checked them. The note says "a few minutes apart" rather
than naming a number, which is the standing rule in voice.md.

### Where the exposure actually sits, and where it does not

**Not in Workhub itself.** Texas industrial real estate, leased in dollars,
built with dollars. No FX.

**Possibly in the family business.** From Workhub's own founding story: Alfredo
inherited **six paper and cardboard box factories** from his father, established
1985, and the story describes them as **multinational** and says he chose to run
them from Houston. His bio adds that before Workhub he "founded and managed
several international companies, specifically in Real Estate, Marketing and
Packaging." Roberto Valenzuela separately built returnable plastic packaging
companies from 2001 and holds a degree from ITESM Monterrey.

**And in the tenant base.** 30+ small companies in Workhub parks, a notable share
Mexican owned, and Workhub Conroe Park North is explicitly marketed to
"foreign-trade logistics, manufacturing, and production."

So he is the same dual shape as Guillermo DeLeon: **a possible account and a
referral channel at once.** The honest thing is that the Workhub real estate is
not the opening and the packaging group might be, and I cannot tell from outside
whether those factories sit under a US entity. **Marcel cannot work a purely
Mexican company, so that is a question to ask, not a claim to make.**

### The note therefore sells the neighborhood, not a thesis

> apart, and we have 17 connections in common, which makes this overdue. I work
> on FX and cross border payments at Monex USA. Easier over coffee than on a
> call. Marcel

**244 chars.** No exposure named on purpose. With a co-founder CFO a mile away

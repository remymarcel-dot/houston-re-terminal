## Why it is written this way

**The conditional does the work.** "If Lasseter buys its French oak
direct" asks a real question without telling a CFO what a CFO already
knows. The version that says "the euro moves between the PO and the
payment" lectures her about her own job.

**The dollar sentence is the strongest line in the email.** Small
wineries often buy through a US importer who quotes in dollars with an FX
margin already inside the price. Telling her that someone else may be
charging her for a risk she thinks she does not have is genuinely useful
and not obvious, and it works whether or not she buys direct. It also
means the email cannot dead end: both branches lead somewhere.

**No claim about what the webinar covered.** She was there. Marcel was
there. Neither of us should assert what it did or did not get to.

**Short, because she is GM and CFO at once.** Two hats at a twenty
person winery means no time. 150 words.

**Nothing about not finding her on LinkedIn.** True, irrelevant, and it
would read as odd.

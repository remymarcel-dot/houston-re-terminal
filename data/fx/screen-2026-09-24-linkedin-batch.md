## The two questions that decided everything

Six well credentialed CFOs came back no for one reason. Gurtz Electric,
Blauch Brothers, Gulf Coast Midstream, Bayou City Energy, WETT,
Grassroots Carbon: **domestic contractors, midstream energy and
utilities**. Dollars in, dollars out, no currency anywhere.

Before a profile is worth the time it needs a yes to one of these:

1. **Does money leave the US entity in a currency that is not dollars?**
   Foreign growers, foreign suppliers, foreign payroll, foreign subsidiaries.
2. **Does money arrive from abroad, and could they invoice in the
   buyer's currency to win business?**

A great CFO where both answers are no is still no. A mediocre seat where
one is yes is worth more.

## Too big, which is a real category

ECOM ($3.09B, Swiss), G-III Apparel ($3B, NASDAQ), DP World (~$20B,
Dubai state owned), Tenneco (Apollo owned), Sephora (LVMH). **The ones
with a real treasury department already have a real treasury solution**,
with ISDAs at relationship banks. Being flattered by the size of the
name is the mistake. Monex's band is roughly $10M to $500M, which is
exactly where Universal Weather at $555M sits at the top edge.

## Where the pitch inverts

Two on the queue need the opposite opener, and getting it wrong loses
them in one line. **Adams Group** and **Leatherman** are exporters. US
exporters almost always invoice in dollars and push the risk onto the
foreign buyer, so "currency is eating your margin" is simply false and
they will say so. The argument is: **let the buyer pay in their own
currency and beat the competitor who makes them carry it.** A revenue
argument, not a risk one.

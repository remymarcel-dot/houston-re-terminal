## Do NOT withdraw it to re-send with a note

That is the tempting move and it is the wrong one. **LinkedIn blocks you from
re-inviting someone for about three weeks after you withdraw an invitation.**

- the pending invitation, which may well be accepted on its own
- three weeks of no LinkedIn contact at all
- all for the sake of 300 characters

A bare invitation from a 2nd degree contact with mutuals gets accepted often
enough that it is worth more sitting there than withdrawn.

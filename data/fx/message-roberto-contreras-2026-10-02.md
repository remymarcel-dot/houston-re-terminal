## The hook is something he did twenty years ago

His About: *"a businessman with strong ties in the United States, Mexico and
Europe... has started and owned several highly successful companies including a
surfacing company that is renowned world-wide, **Cosentino North America**."*

Cosentino is the Spanish surfacing group out of Almería, Silestone and Dekton.
**He built its North American arm.** That is years of buying Spanish product in
euros and selling it into the US in dollars.

**And now he is doing it again.** Moderno Porcelain Works sells large format
porcelain slab, which comes from Spain and Italy. Same trade, same currency pair,
second time around.

So the note does not explain the exposure. It tells him we noticed he has run
this exact problem before, which is the most flattering true thing available and
the one nobody else writing to him will have found.

Marcel's sequence is right: go at the money first, keep the network in reserve.

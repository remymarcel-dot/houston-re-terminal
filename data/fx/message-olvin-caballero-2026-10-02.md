and household products across Central America.

**Four of those employees work in Greater Houston.** Olvin is the head of

## What the note does not claim

His profile is nearly empty: no photo, no About, one liked post, no mutuals
shown. So the note **cannot** reference anything personal, and anything invented
would be obvious.

It leans entirely on structure instead, which is the honest material: the size of
the parent, the size of the Houston presence, and what that necessarily means
about his desk. **It does not name a currency.** Honduras uses the lempira, but
whether Dinant's US entity settles in lempiras, dollars, or both is unknown from
outside, and guessing it to a head of accounting would be the one way to lose
him in a line.

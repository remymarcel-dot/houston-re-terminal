## Why the existing connections mostly do not help

32 exhibitors have a connection, 105 people, only 28 of them decision
makers. Almost all fail the rules fixed this week:

- **Mexican or LATAM seats at global groups.** Siemens México, Nexteer
  CFO Mexico, Schneider CFO Argentina, Electrolux Andean, Aptiv LATAM.
  Those people bank in their own country, which makes them Monex México's
  or out of region entirely.

  Electrolux, Aptiv, Nexteer all run their own treasury with bank FX lines.
- **Direct competitors.** Banco Base is a Mexican FX bank, its Director de
  Desarrollo de Negocio FX y Derivados is an exhibitor. Kapital is a
  Mexican fintech bank. Viva Capital is an El Paso factoring company.
  Do not approach any of them as prospects.
- **Already handled.** Grupo Palco, Carlos Palma was messaged Sep 21.
  Inclusive Logistics, Judith Torres Cruz is marked do not contact after
  three unanswered touches.

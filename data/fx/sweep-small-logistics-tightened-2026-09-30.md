## What it costs to make this actionable

Surnames are masked on the free search, so nothing here is sendable yet.

- **Free route:** Marcel finds each on LinkedIn from first name, title and
  company. That is what HeyReach needs anyway, since it requires a real
  profile URL.

  is six credits. That returns the surname, the email and the direct dial.

**For this segment the paid route may be the better one.** The Dean Rigg
lesson from an hour ago applies: a CFO at a small carrier had **one** LinkedIn
connection, and email and phone were the only real channels. Small freight
companies live on the phone. Enriching the six and calling them may beat
waiting on connection requests that never get opened.

# Mesilla Valley Transportation: yes, with one caveat that matters more than size

Marcel asked on 2026-09-30 whether MVT has FX potential. Short answer: **yes,
and it is a better fit than the revenue line suggests.** Not in the repo
anywhere before today, and **not on the El Paso summit exhibitor directory**
either, which I screened at 173 names.

## The FX exposure is real, not manufactured

- Founded 1982 in Las Cruces by Royal Jones and Jimmy Ray. **$639.1M revenue
  in 2026**, 2,000 drivers, 2,500 trailers.
- **The largest privately owned transportation fleet in the United States.**
- Trailers cross into Mexico **practically every hour of the day**, and the
  fleet also delivers into **Canada**.
- **MVT Logistics** runs cross border intermodal, drayage, truckload,
  brokerage, warehousing and distribution.

A US carrier running Mexico lanes at that volume pays Mexican carriers,
drayage partners, fuel, tolls and repairs on the southern side, and Canada
delivery adds CAD. That is a continuous multi currency payables book, which
is the opposite of the manufactured angle the screen usually has to reject.

## Size: above the band, and the usual conclusion does not follow

$639M is past the roughly $10M to $500M band, and past Universal Weather at
$555M, which the files call the top edge.

**But the size test applies to the decision structure, not to the revenue
line.** That is the correction written into icp.md earlier today after the
Ortiz Rodea cut was overturned. A $639M **founder run private company** is
structurally different from a $639M public one:

- No investor relations, no SEC reporting, no quarterly optics
- Still run by the two men who drove the first trucks, 44 years on
- A decision can be made by one person rather than by a committee

So the reason to hesitate is not the revenue. It is the next paragraph.

## The real caveat: this is a displacement sale, not a greenfield one

A company that has been crossing into Mexico hourly for forty years has had
somebody solving this since the 1980s. Whatever they do today is entrenched,
and Apollo confirms it: **they carry a dedicated Cash Management and Finance
seat.** At $639M with a person whose job is literally cash management, bank
FX lines almost certainly exist.

That cuts both ways. The conversation will be with someone who understands
the product immediately, which is good. And the incumbent is the obstacle,
not the absence of need.

**Which means the Cavazos lesson applies from the first message.** Marcel,
2026-09-29, on Jorge Cavazos: *"why we do not give an aditional try and say
that we are very competitive and might improved the fx rate that he is
getting now, if he give us chance to comapare is worth trying and if we can
not improve we leave it there."* A concept pitch will bounce here. A rate
comparison on a recent USD MXN fill will not.

## Worth an invitation, ranked

**Recently hired** is on his Sales Navigator key signals. Cibes is a Swedish
lift manufacturer; he runs the North America entity's finance. Lifts are built
in Sweden and sold in dollars, so the cost base is in kronor and euros and the
revenue is not. This is exactly the Pablo Ortiz Rodea shape: a regional finance
chief under a European parent, running a market rather than reporting into a
Stockholm treasury. New in seat is the strongest trigger we have.

### 2. Martin Hauser, VP Finance, Koenig & Bauer (US)/(CA), Dallas
**Recently hired**, also on the key signals. Koenig & Bauer is a German press
manufacturer; he carries both the US and Canadian entities. Presses are built in
Würzburg and invoiced in euros. Canada is in scope, so his seat spans two
currencies against one euro payables stream. 353 connections and four mutuals,
so the network is thin, which usually means an invitation rather than a
message gets read.

### 3. Jorge Kovacs Morayta, CEO, Klass Time LTD, Greater Houston
Klass is the Mexican aguas frescas brand. CEO seat, your own city, ITAM
educated, 52 mutual connections. Mexican product moving into the US grocery
trade is the thesis that has converted most often in this book. Write in
Spanish.

first degree a message costs nothing to find out.

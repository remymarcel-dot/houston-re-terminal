## The hook, and why it is better than a normal produce angle

His 28 September post: **"About to start 500 new acres of Kaliroy Shade House
Roma Tomatoes."**

Most produce notes have to open on routine payables, which every competitor also
says. This one opens on a **capital project with a size, a schedule and a budget**,
which is a different and much better conversation:

- A 500 acre shade house build is months of structures, labor and materials.
- That spend is in pesos. The revenue funding it arrives in dollars in the US.
- A build is budgeted at a rate set **now** and paid at rates set **later**.

That gap does not show up as an FX line. **It shows up as the build coming in
over budget**, which is the line in the message doing the real work, because it
is how he will have experienced it himself on previous expansions.

Routine payables are a rate you accept. A project budget is a number someone is
accountable for. The second one gets answered.

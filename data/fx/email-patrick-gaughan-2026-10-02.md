He arrived through the El Paso Summit exhibitor list and I flagged that a
Cleveland metal stamper on a Mexico nearshoring list looked like a weak
addition. **That was wrong, and their own published history says why.**

From Universal Metal Products' timeline page:

> **2003** → Appliance OEM's step up migration from US based operations into

**The border plant exists because their customers moved into Mexico.** That is
the nearshoring shape exactly, and it means the El Paso summit list had him
right and my doubt was the error.

## Where I am NOT going to guess

A US manufacturer with a border plant serving customers who produce in Mexico
**usually** has something denominated the other way: a receivable, a supplier, or
the logistics between. **Whether UMP actually settles anything in pesos is not
visible from outside**, and after the Nate Ray reasoning the right move is to
ask rather than assert. The question is also the qualifier, so either answer ends
the uncertainty in one reply.

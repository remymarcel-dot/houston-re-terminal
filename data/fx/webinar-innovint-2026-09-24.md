### The rule this batch produced

Four databases agreeing that a person is not at a company is evidence.
One database having them at low confidence is not. When the low
confidence record is the only one and the name is common, the LinkedIn
URL is the part to distrust, not the person. Check the company People
list before spending the invitation.

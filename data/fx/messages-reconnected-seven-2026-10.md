> Kevin, I said in September I would explain how that gets unpicked, so here it is.

>
> When a factory quotes you in dollars it has picked a rate to protect itself and built
> a cushion into the unit price. You cannot see it because it is not a line item, it is
> inside the number. Three steps make it visible.
>
> One. Ask the factory to quote the same SKU in RMB alongside the USD price. Convert the
> RMB figure at the day's rate. The gap is the cushion.

> who is pricing the dollar aggressively and who is not.
>
> Three. Once you can see it, it becomes a choice: pay in RMB and carry the risk
> yourself, which is usually cheaper, or leave it with them knowing what it costs.

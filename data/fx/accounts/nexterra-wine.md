## Why the flow is worth more than the headline number

Import records show **407 bills of lading between July 2019 and July
2026, with 10 in the last 30 days and 22 in the last 90.** That is the
detail that matters. It is not a handful of large annual buys; it is a
rhythm of roughly two to three shipments a week.

A recurring cadence is a hedging program, not a spot trade. It means
forwards, a budget rate to defend, and a reason to talk every month
rather than once a year. If the $4M is anywhere near right, that is
$4M repeating annually against a cost base they have to quote to private
label customers in advance.
